There is a Tuesday-to-Friday publication lag. Use the Legacy report format exclusively (Commercial, Non-Commercial, Non-Reportable categories) as the Dreesmann methodology was developed on Legacy data.

Critical data fields you MUST ingest (not optional — every single one is required for signal calculations):

Non-Commercial Long/Short: Total long/short futures positions held by speculators, hedge funds, CTAs. Primary signal driver.
Commercial Long/Short: Total long/short futures positions held by producers, merchants, processors, users. Inverse signal to speculators.

Change in Commercial Long/Short: Week-over-week change. Hedging momentum.

Data normalization rules: Convert all positions to contracts (not notional value). Detect and handle contract roll weeks where OI drops >15%. Note CFTC reporting threshold changes (2014, 2020) and test whether they materially affect signal calculations.

3. THE DREESMANN 5-CONDITION SIGNAL: EXACT SPECIFICATION

Five independent conditions. Signal grade determined by how many are simultaneously satisfied.

Condition 1 — Commercial Sentiment Index (CSI):
CSI = (Net Commercial Position - Min Net Commercial over lookback) / (Max Net Commercial over lookback - Min Net Commercial over lookback)
Where Net Commercial = Commercial Long - Commercial Short. Default lookback: 156 weeks (~3 years), must be configurable.
CSI > 0.70 = extreme commercial long (bullish signal for Gold)
CSI < 0.30 = extreme commercial short (bearish signal for Gold)

Condition 2 — Non-Commercial Positioning Percentage:
NC Long % = Non-Commercial Long / Total Open Interest

40% = extreme speculative long (bearish signal)
< 15% = extreme speculative short (bullish signal)
Thresholds configurable for sensitivity analysis.

Condition 3 — Non-Reportable Sentiment Index (NRSI):
NRSI = (Net Non-Reportable - Min over lookback) / (Max over lookback - Min over lookback)
NRSI < 0.30 = small traders extremely net short (contrarian bullish)
NRSI > 0.70 = small traders extremely net long (contrarian bearish)
Lookback matches CSI (default 156 weeks).

Condition 4 — Open Interest Trend Confirmation:
For bullish signal (CSI > 0.70): total OI must be increasing WoW OR 4-week MA of OI rising.
For bearish signal (CSI < 0.30): same OI condition.
Falling OI = condition NOT satisfied.

Condition 5 — Week-over-Week Change Confirmation:
Bullish: WoW change in Commercial Long positive AND WoW change in Non-Commercial Short positive.
Bearish: WoW change in Commercial Short positive AND WoW change in Non-Commercial Long positive.

Signal Grading Matrix:

Grade
Conditions
Trading Implication
A+	5/5	Full position, max risk
A	4/5	Full position, standard risk
B+	3/5	Reduced position
B	2/5	Half position, tight risk
C	0-1	No trade

CRITICAL: Store which specific conditions fired as boolean array [c1,c2,c3,c4,c5] for every signal at every point in time. Essential for regime analysis.

4. BACKTEST ENGINE: CORE ARCHITECTURE

Data Pipeline (3 stages):
Stage 1 — Data Ingestion: Pull historical COT data for Gold, EUR, GBP from Jan 2000 to now. Cache locally (JSON or SQLite).
Stage 2 — Price Data Alignment: Pull historical weekly closes for XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD. Align to TUESDAY COT report date (not Friday publication). Sources: Yahoo Finance, Alpha Vantage.
Stage 3 — Signal Calculation: Run 5-condition engine on every historical week.

Signal-to-Price Mapping:
Signal date = COT Tuesday report date.
Entry price = weekly close of the week AFTER signal generation (simulates execution after Friday publication lag).
Forward return windows: exactly 1W, 4W, 8W, 12W, 26W after entry.
For each window record: % return, win/loss, MFE (max favorable excursion), MAE (max adverse excursion).

Caching: Cache COT data aggressively (only refetch most recent 4 weeks). Signal results cached by parameter set. Price data cached indefinitely. Display "Last Updated" timestamp.

Computation: ~3,750 data points × 5 conditions × 156-week lookback = ~2.9M operations. Use Web Workers or chunked processing. Show progress bar during calculation.

5. PHASE 1: HISTORICAL CORRELATION STUDY

Calculate these metrics broken down by signal grade × instrument × holding period (3-dimensional matrix):

Metric
Definition

11. ACADEMIC STANDARDS AND COMPARISON METHODOLOGY

Dreesmann (2012) Replication: Calculate same metrics Dreesmann reported (cumulative return, annualized return/vol, Sharpe, max drawdown, win %). Display your numbers side-by-side with his published figures. Investigate and explain any significant differences.

Zhang & Laws (2020) Verification: Calculate Pearson correlation between CSI and contemporaneous Gold returns. Report: correlation coefficient, regression beta, standard error, t-statistic, p-value. Their finding: beta = -0.016, t-stat = -5.696, p < 0.01. Verify this holds in your dataset. This is the "verify with research" requirement.

Out-of-Sample Protocol: Split 20-year data: 70% in-sample (2000-2014) for development, 30% out-of-sample (2015-2025) for validation only. Report metrics separately. Out-of-sample Sharpe must be ≥50% of in-sample Sharpe for robustness.

12. TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS AND EDGE CASES

Contract Roll Handling: Detect rolls (OI drops >15% WoW). Use combined futures-and-options data (smooths roll artifacts). Flag roll weeks.

CFTC Threshold Changes: Note 2014 and 2020 threshold change dates. Implement Chow test for structural breaks. If break detected, split into sub-periods and report separately.

Look-Ahead Bias Prevention: First 156 weeks don't have full lookback history. Either exclude them or use expanding window (start 52 weeks, expand to 156). NEVER use 156-week lookback from week 1.

Multiple Testing Correction: With 5 grades × 3 instruments × 5 holding periods = 75 tests. Apply Bonferroni correction (significance level = 0.05/75 = 0.00067) or Benjamini-Hochberg FDR. Report both corrected and uncorrected p-values.

Performance Degradation Monitor: Rolling 3-year Sharpe windows. If recent 3-year Sharpe < 50% of long-term average, display warning: "Signal performance may be degrading."
