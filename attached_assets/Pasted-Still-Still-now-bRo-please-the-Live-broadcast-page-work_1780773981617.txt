Planning AirPods behavior integration
Here's exactly what was fixed and what you'll experience now:

Why your AirPods were disconnecting — the real cause:

Chromebook has to switch AirPods between two Bluetooth modes:

A2DP — high-quality stereo for listening (what they use when just connected)
HFP — lower-quality headset mode with microphone (what streaming needs)
This profile switch is done at the OS level — the browser can't skip it. The disconnect was happening because the app was accidentally triggering the switch twice in rapid succession:

Once when loading the device list (to get device names)
Again when you clicked Arm
Two rapid profile switches = AirPods gave up and fully disconnected.

What's fixed:

Fix 1 — Eliminated the double-switch: Device list loading now checks if mic permission is already granted. If yes, it skips the unnecessary getUserMedia call that was causing the first switch.

Fix 2 — Auto-reconnect retry: The arm now expects AirPods to briefly disconnect during the single remaining profile switch. It watches for the Bluetooth devicechange event, waits up to 12 seconds per attempt, and automatically re-arms once AirPods come back. Up to 3 attempts total.

Fix 3 — Removed forced 44100Hz audio rate: The old AudioContext({ sampleRate: 44100 }) was making ChromeOS negotiate a specific audio format on top of the profile switch, which made BT stacks reject the HFP session. Now uses the system's native rate.

What you'll see now when arming:

Click "Arm Bluetooth Mic"
AirPods may still briefly disconnect (5-10s) — this is still normal, it's the OS switching modes
The button shows "Switching to mic mode — AirPods may disconnect briefly, auto-retrying…"
When AirPods come back, it arms automatically → "✅ Bluetooth Mic Armed & Ready"
Click Go Live — AirPods stay connected throughout the stream
