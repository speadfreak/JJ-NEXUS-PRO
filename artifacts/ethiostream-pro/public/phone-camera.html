🟢 Background Mode Active

Your camera is staying live in the background. Audio session is active. You can minimize your browser — the stream will continue on Android Chrome.

📋 How to use

1

Tap Rear HD Cam or Front Cam — allow camera when asked

2

Tap 🎤 Mic & Audio — select your AirPods if connected

3

A Session ID appears — on the Chromebook go to Studio → Phone Camera and tap ⚡ Quick Reconnect or enter the code

4

Wait for "Connected" — your phone is now a live HD camera + clean mic in the studio

5

Android: You can minimize Chrome — background audio keeps the stream alive. iOS: Install to Home Screen for best background support.
